## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales and stock performance of
Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market over a
14-day period. Wanjiku needs to understand how her business
is performing, whether sales are growing, which days
generate the highest revenue, and which products require
restocking. The analysis uses core Python programming
concepts, including lists, dictionaries, functions,
loops, conditional statements and string formatting.
The findings are used to provide practical recommendations
for improving sales performance, inventory management
and product profitability.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Positive sales growth:** Weekly sales increased
   from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2,
   representing 10.66% growth.

2. **Stronger weekend performance:** Average weekend
   sales were KES 4,900 compared to weekday sales of
   KES 2,685, an increase of approximately 82.50%.

3. **Stock replenishment:** Tomatoes (18 kg) and
   Sukuma (9 kg) were below their respective reorder
   levels of 25 kg and 15 kg.
